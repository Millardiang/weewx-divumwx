DivumWX - Windy Radar card: play once, then stop (for 2.0.0)
===========================================================

Goes on top of version-2.0-beta at 60c2613 (your "beta2" commit, which
is the v4 location fix).

What changed
  - The radar plays through once whenever the card or the larger map
    loads, then stops on a still map:
      card:       on page load, every 15 minutes, and on a unit change
      larger map: when it opens, and on each tab (Radar, Satellite, ...)
    Windy's map can't be paused from the dashboard, so it's loaded with
    play=true and, after PLAY_ONCE_SECONDS, swapped for the same map
    without play. The still map loads underneath the playing one and
    takes over once it's ready, so there's no flash.
  - If a visitor clicks into the map while it plays, it's left playing
    for them.
  - Title tooltip: "The radar plays once when it loads; press [play] on
    the map to play it again." The larger map's footer says the same.
  - The v4 location fix is unchanged.

Setting the play time
  Windy's playback speed can't be set from the dashboard, so the stop is
  timed. PLAY_ONCE_SECONDS = 15 is set in two places:
    divumwx/cardsBundleNew.js     near the top of the cardWindyRadar module
    divumwx/modalWindyRadar.html  near "var MODAL_ZOOM = 8;"
  It counts from the moment Windy's page loads, so include the second or
  two Windy takes to fetch the radar. Time one run of the loop on your
  site and change both. Editing cardsBundleNew.js changes its contents,
  so also update the ?v= on its <script> tag in index.html,
  hyperIndex.html and lexendIndex.html (any new value works), or browsers
  may keep the old copy.

Files are laid out at their repo paths. These 6 are the only ones that
differ from your branch; copy each over the version in your repo:
  divumwx/cardsBundleNew.js        cardWindyRadar: play once, location fix
  divumwx/modalWindyRadar.html     play once, location fix, coords shown
  divumwx/index.html               bundle ?v=780323d9
  divumwx/hyperIndex.html          bundle ?v=780323d9
  divumwx/lexendIndex.html         bundle ?v=780323d9
  CHANGELOG.md                     entry updated

Using git instead, on version-2.0-beta (60c2613):
  git apply windy-radar-card.patch

Reminder: the repo root now has CHANGES.txt and windy-radar-card.patch
from earlier packages. The installer copies every file in the repo root
into users' WeeWX folder, so you'll probably want to delete both from
the repo rather than replace them.
