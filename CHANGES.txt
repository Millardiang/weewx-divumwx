DivumWX - Windy Radar card: amber station pin (for 2.0.0)
=========================================================

Goes on top of version-2.0-beta at ecec5cd (your v6 "Windy" commit).

What changed
  - The station marker is now an amber map pin, with its tip exactly at
    the centre of the card and of the larger map, where Windy centres on
    the station's coordinates. It replaces v6's red dot: red is also a
    heavy-rain colour on the radar, and a pin shape can't be mistaken
    for rain or for Windy's own pulsing blue dot.
  - Windy's blue dot is NOT the station: it's each viewer's location as
    Windy guesses it from their internet address. It can't be moved or
    hidden from the dashboard, so it will keep appearing wherever Windy
    guesses (for visitors elsewhere, over them).
  - The title tooltip and the larger map's footer now say "The amber pin
    marks the weather station."
  - As before, the pin hides once a visitor clicks into the map (it would
    no longer be on the station) and comes back with the next load.

Files are laid out at their repo paths. These 6 are the only ones that
differ from your branch; copy each over the version in your repo:
  divumwx/cardsBundleNew.js        amber station pin, tooltip
  divumwx/modalWindyRadar.html     amber station pin, footer note
  divumwx/index.html               bundle ?v=612e8cbf
  divumwx/hyperIndex.html          bundle ?v=612e8cbf
  divumwx/lexendIndex.html         bundle ?v=612e8cbf
  CHANGELOG.md                     entry updated

Using git instead, on version-2.0-beta (ecec5cd):
  git apply windy-radar-card.patch

Clean-up: the repo root now has CHANGES.txt, windy-radar-card.patch and
windy-test.html from earlier packages. The installer copies every file in
the repo root into users' WeeWX folder, so you'll probably want to delete
all three from the repo.
