DivumWX - Windy Radar card: whole card opens the larger map (for 2.0.0)
=======================================================================

Goes on top of version-2.0-beta at da661b7 (your v7 "Windy" commit).

What changed (card only; the larger map is unchanged)
  - The title is plain text again, not a link.
  - Clicking anywhere on the card opens the larger Windy map, like the
    other cards. The card's map is look-only again, so clicks and drags
    act on the card.
  - Hovering over the card shows:
      Click to open the larger Windy map.
      The amber pin marks the weather station.
      Click and hold to drag and drop cards into preferred position
  - Unchanged: the radar plays once on each load then stops, the amber
    pin marks the station at the centre, and the larger map is still
    interactive (pan, zoom, press play again).

Files are laid out at their repo paths. These 5 are the only ones that
differ from your branch; copy each over the version in your repo:
  divumwx/cardsBundleNew.js        cardWindyRadar: whole-card link, tooltip
  divumwx/index.html               bundle ?v=f3207917
  divumwx/hyperIndex.html          bundle ?v=f3207917
  divumwx/lexendIndex.html         bundle ?v=f3207917
  CHANGELOG.md                     entry updated

Using git instead, on version-2.0-beta (da661b7):
  git apply windy-radar-card.patch

Clean-up: the repo root has CHANGES.txt, windy-radar-card.patch and
windy-test.html from earlier packages. The installer copies every file in
the repo root into users' WeeWX folder, so you'll probably want to delete
all three from the repo.
