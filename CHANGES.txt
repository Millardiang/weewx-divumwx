DivumWX - Windy Radar card: confirmed link + station dot (for 2.0.0)
====================================================================

Goes on top of version-2.0-beta at 3d4bf5a (your v5 "beta2" commit).

What changed
  - The card and the larger map now send Windy exactly the link you
    confirmed centres on the station. v5 also sent unit settings
    (metricRain/metricTemp/metricWind), the only difference from your
    working link, so those are gone and Windy picks its own units.
    The card now asks for:
      https://embed.windy.com/embed.html?type=map&location=coordinates
        &zoom=7&overlay=radar&level=surface&lat=51.937&lon=-0.982
        &message=true&play=true
    and the still map after one play is the same without &play=true.
  - Station dot: a red dot drawn by DivumWX at the centre of the card
    and the larger map, where the station is when the map loads. It
    hides when a visitor clicks into the map (it would no longer be on
    the station) and comes back with the next load.
  - Play once, then stop: unchanged from v5 (PLAY_ONCE_SECONDS = 15).
    Windy's playback speed still can't be set from the dashboard.

If anything is still off-centre: windy-test.html
  Copy windy-test.html into your site's divumwx folder (next to
  index.html) and open it in your browser. It shows four Windy maps of
  your station side by side, each one change away from your working
  link:
    A  the link you confirmed
    B  A without play=true      (what the still map uses)
    C  A plus the unit settings (what v5 sent)
    D  A plus marker=true and detailLat/detailLon
  Each has a red dot where the station should be. Tell me which maps are
  off-centre and where any Windy marker appears. Delete the file after.

Files are laid out at their repo paths. These 6 are the only ones that
differ from your branch; copy each over the version in your repo:
  divumwx/cardsBundleNew.js        confirmed link, station dot
  divumwx/modalWindyRadar.html     confirmed link, station dot
  divumwx/index.html               bundle ?v=238da6db
  divumwx/hyperIndex.html          bundle ?v=238da6db
  divumwx/lexendIndex.html         bundle ?v=238da6db
  CHANGELOG.md                     entry updated
windy-test.html is only for testing; it isn't part of DivumWX.

Using git instead, on version-2.0-beta (3d4bf5a):
  git apply windy-radar-card.patch

Reminder: the repo root has CHANGES.txt and windy-radar-card.patch from
earlier packages. The installer copies every file in the repo root into
users' WeeWX folder, so you'll probably want to delete both.
