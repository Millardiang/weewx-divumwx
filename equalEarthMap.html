Equal Earth (Šavrič, Jenny & Patterson, 2018) keeps every country at its true relative size, so “map size vs equator” reads ×1.00 wherever you click. Switch to Mercator and click Greenland, Russia or Canada to see how much a conformal map inflates high latitudes. Tissot circles are all 500 km in radius on the ground: Equal Earth keeps their area and stretches their shape toward the edges, Mercator keeps them round and lets them grow. The day/night shading is drawn from the Sun's position at the current time and refreshes every minute. Drag sideways or use the arrow keys to rotate. Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:110m; areas computed on a sphere of radius 6,371 km.
