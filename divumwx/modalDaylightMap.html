The Equal Earth projection (Šavrič, Jenny & Patterson, 2018) shows every country at its true relative size. Shading marks night and the civil, nautical and astronomical twilight bands; the Sun and Moon tracks trace the points they pass directly over for one full trip round the Earth, with a dot for each hour. Click a country for its true area and its size on the map compared with the same area at the equator: ×1.00 everywhere on Equal Earth, while Mercator inflates high latitudes (Greenland about ×16). Tissot circles are all 500 km in radius on the ground. Drag sideways or use the arrow keys to move the central meridian; Auto view keeps the station, and the Sun and Moon when they are up there, near the middle. The time slider moves the Sun and Moon up to 12 hours either side of now; everything updates every 5 seconds. For the globe, open Astronomy → Equal Earth Map. Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:110m.
